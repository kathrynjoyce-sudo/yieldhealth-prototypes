What We Do

Two lines of business, one operating premise: prove the outcome and prove the return, in the same breath.

Value-based care asks a hospital or provider group to manage a patient's full course of care and be accountable for what it costs. Yield Healthcare builds the clinical coordination and the financial tracking as one system, not two.
Episode-Based
TEAM Episode Management
Support for hospitals navigating CMS's mandatory Transforming Episode Accountability Model — care coordination for the episode window, target-price and reconciliation tracking, and hospital and post-acute vendor performance reporting across the mandated episode types.
Population-Based
PMPM Population Care Management
A per-member-per-month care management program for risk-bearing provider groups and ACOs, spanning post-acute transitions, peri-operative optimization, and complex chronic care management across a commercial and Medicare Advantage population.
How We Work

A graduated path, not a single product.

A hospital or provider group can enter anywhere along the ladder, and move up only once the case for a deeper relationship is proven on its own data.
Tier One
Advisory
A fixed-fee diagnostic and strategy engagement — a straight answer on episode readiness and financial exposure, with no shared-savings component and no long-term commitment required to start.
Tier Two
Platform
Technology and remote care-navigation priced against savings — episode dashboards, target-price tracking, and a managed navigator model for hospitals ready to operationalize episode performance.
Tier Three
Embedded
RN care managers and patient navigators placed on-site — pre-op clinic through post-discharge follow-up — for systems that want boots on the ground and a deeper, billing-integrated relationship.
